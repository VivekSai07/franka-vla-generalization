# G0: the generalization benchmark, arms A2 and A0 (2026-11-10)

G0 measures what P2's policy and A2 (P2's data plus force in the state) do when one thing about the scene changes. Five axes,
each trial paired with the same layout under nominal conditions. Measured, not sealed. Spec:
[2026-11-06-g0-generalization-benchmark-design.md](superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-g0-generalization-benchmark-design.md) ·
plan: [2026-11-06-g0-generalization-benchmark.md](superpowers/plans/2026-11-06-g0-generalization-benchmark.md).

## Headline

**No axis is held by either arm.** An axis is held iff the paired drop's 95% bootstrap interval has an upper bound of at most
10 points (pre-registered, D13). **Spatial collapses**: A2 scores 4/142 (2.8%) and A0 3/142 against nominal 91.5% and 79.6%,
at every one of the four spatial levels. Distractors (three to five, P2 trained on one or two) cost A2 36 points. Lighting and
background cost much less than predicted.

## Failure classes (cause of the first failure, mutually exclusive)

A2, pooled over the axis: spatial never lifted 131, wrong object 6, timeout carrying 1; distractors never lifted 53, wrong object 6,
dropped outside 2, timeout carrying 2; lighting never lifted 30, wrong object 4; background never lifted 17, dropped outside 2,
timeout carrying 2, wrong object 1 (no wrong bin); disturbance never lifted 64, timeout carrying 1, wrong object 1.
A0 adds failure classes A2 mostly lacks: **timeout carrying** (12 nominal, 17 lighting, 14 background, 11 disturbance) and **wrong bin**
(6 under background; 5 of them at `both`).
Grasp-and-lift (the sub-metric, target lifted at least 5 cm at any tick): A2 spatial 6/142 against 132/142 nominal.

**Trap in the `disturbance` rows.** `never_lifted` is computed as in P2's analysis: the target never rose 2 cm and stayed for 15 ticks.
On `drop` trials the policy lifted the target (grasp-and-lift 66/66) and the harness released it 5 cm up, so most of the 24 "never
lifted" are *failed recoveries*, not failed grasps. On `move` trials grasp-and-lift is 29/64, which is the policy failing to re-acquire
a target that moved 8 cm when the hand came within 6 cm. The `recovered` counts (success after the disturbance fired) are A2 73 of 133
fired, A0 68 of 131. The disturbance did not fire in 7 (A2) and 9 (A0) of 140 trials.

## What is and is not established

- Established: the paired drops above, per arm, for this checkpoint, recipe and one training seed; layouts are the independent
  units; trials are one per layout per axis.
- Spatial: the total collapse is uniform across all four levels (0, 0, 2, 2 of ~35), so it is not layout concentration. All 15
  dropped trials come from lemon layouts the scripted expert could not grasp; the other 849 are expert-feasible under their own
  condition, so the spatial scenes are solvable.
- **Exploratory, not validated, mechanism hypothesis for spatial.** The median lowest hand height (forward kinematics of the
  recorded state, no positive control beyond the nominal rows) is 0.213 m at nominal and 0.216-0.220 m at every spatial level: the
  hand goes to the same absolute height whatever the table height, which is what a joint-space policy that never saw table height
  would do. A hand-frame grasp analysis and the videos would be needed before calling it a cause.
- Not tested: why `low_angle` is the worst lighting level, why `tinted` costs nothing, and why distractors at five cost more for A2
  than A0. Each is a hypothesis for the data, not a finding.
- A2 and A0 use different checkpoints (88,830 and 101,520: M0's primary choice for A2, the bundle's for A0), not matched phase.
- Distractor counts three to five go beyond P2's one or two, so that axis mixes "more objects" with "more than trained".
- The `move` destination was close to the start for 12 of 144 layouts (trigger at tick 0), kept as designed.
- A3 was not run (not asked). The selection tier was not run for claims (D14).

## Implications for G1

G1's target is the pre-registered one: every axis held, interval upper bound at most 10 points. Ordered by distance from it:
spatial (about 89 points; table height and start region must be in the data, as GraspVLA's randomization says), distractors (about
36; more objects than P2's one or two), disturbance (about 39; `move` is the hard half), lighting (about 17; `low_angle` first),
background (about 7; nearest to held). Nominal for G1's policy must stay near A2's 91.5% or the comparison is not paired with this table.

Reproduce: `scripts/gen_eval.sh` (header lists the steps); the run used `plan`, `feasibility 12 3`, `identity_gate`, `prepare A2|A0`,
`smoke A2`, `benchmark A2|A0`, `compare A2|A0`, `final`.
